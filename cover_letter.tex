\opening{Dear Guest Editors,}

Please find enclosed our manuscript, ``A Compact 11 GHz Split Cavity for Rapid RF Evaluation of Superconducting Films in a Standard PPMS,'' for the ASC 2026 special issue of the \emph{IEEE Transactions on Applied Superconductivity}. This work was presented at ASC 2026 as 1MPo2D-06.

We report a compact two-piece copper cavity that fits in the 26\,mm sample chamber of a standard PPMS. A sputtered Nb film reached $Q_0 = 1.6\times10^{6}$ at 2\,K, and because the cavity opens, we inspected the same surface we RF-tested, linking the measured loss directly to film defects.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has been approved by all authors.
